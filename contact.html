About AutoLL-2

AutoLL-2 is an independent GPL-3.0-only fork of BG1, built for experimenting with Lightning Lane Multi Pass monitoring, planning, and notification workflows.

It is based on the work of Joel Face's BG1 and jgeurts/bg1. AutoLL-2 preserves their license and attribution; it is not endorsed by or associated with Disney.

Source code, issue tracking, and release history are available on the AutoLL-2 GitHub repository.
